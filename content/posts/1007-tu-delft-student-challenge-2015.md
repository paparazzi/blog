Students at the [TU Delft MavLab](http://mavlab.lr.tudelft.nl) compete with ARDrones running [Paparazzi](http://paparazzi.enac.fr) to fly seamlessly around obstacles without injuring the aircraft. The object to autonomously avoid all obstacles. This is a fantastic demonstration of the flexibility of the Paparazzi system and its ability to fly accurately around waypoints.

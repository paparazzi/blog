[![screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-44-14-pm-png](/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-44-14-pm-png-300x225.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-44-14-pm-png.png)Parrot has released their new Bebop 2 camera quadcopter. Paparazzi UAV already has [support for that new platform](https://github.com/paparazzi/paparazzi/pull/1429).

[![screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-43-39-pm-png](/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-43-39-pm-png-300x225.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-43-39-pm-png.png) Thanks to the great work of the [TU-Delft MavLab](http://mavlab.lr.tudelft.nl/) team and especially [Freek van Tienen](https://github.com/fvantienen) it is possible to replace the stock functionality of the Parrot Bebop 2 with Paparazzi UAV.

This change gives the platform a lot of autonomous flight enhancements like dynamic flight plans, as well as improved stabilization algorithms like the INDI controller.

We hope you will enjoy converting your  [![screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-43-16-pm-png](/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-43-16-pm-png-300x225.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/screen-shot-2015-11-17-at-2-43-16-pm-png.png)Bebop 2 into a serious robotic aircraft. :)

For more information you can refer to our [Bebop wiki page](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Bebop).

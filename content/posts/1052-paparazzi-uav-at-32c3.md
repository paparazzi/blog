This year we will be having another meetup at the [32nd Chaos Communication Congress](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2015/wiki/Main_Page) (32C3) in Hamburg, Germany. The event is happening from December 27th to 30th, 2015. Make sure to secure your [tickets](https://events.ccc.de/tag/tickets/)! When you are in the area, [find us](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2015/wiki/Assembly:PaparazziUAV) and say hi! We will have our [Assembly](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2015/wiki/Assembly:PaparazziUAV) there and will have lot's of cool hardware and people with us. See you there! :D

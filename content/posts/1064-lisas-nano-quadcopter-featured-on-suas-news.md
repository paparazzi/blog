[![LadyLisa_Assembled_Kit_5_adjusted_1024x1024](/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/LadyLisa_Assembled_Kit_5_adjusted_1024x1024-300x199.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/LadyLisa_Assembled_Kit_5_adjusted_1024x1024.jpg)In the light of the recent FAA registration limits, the great guys at [sUAS News wrote an article featuring the Paparazzi UAV powered Lisa/S nano Quadcopter](http://www.suasnews.com/2015/12/40814/still-want-gps-flight-but-dont-want-to-register/ "GPS flight without registration"). This platform was developed by [1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.com) in collaboration with the [TU-Delft MAV lab team](http://www.lr.tudelft.nl/en/cooperation/facilities/mav-laboratory/ "MAV Lab website"), provides all the autonomous capabilities while being way below the FAA regulated limits for registration. The [Lisa/S nano Quadcopter](http://1bitsquared.com/products/lisa-s-nano-quadcopter-kit "Lisa/S nano Quadcopter product page") weighs only 39g while the FAA requires all aircraft above 250g to be registred.

Check out [their article](http://www.suasnews.com/2015/12/40814/still-want-gps-flight-but-dont-want-to-register/) for more information.

![IMG_2974](/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/IMG_2974-300x225.jpg)Just in time for the new year [1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.com) releases a new [GPS module named G0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/g0-gps). It is designed to neatly fit on top of the [Paparazzi UAV Elle0 Autopilot](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Elle0), that they released just a few weeks ago.

G0 features the UBlox Max-7Q chipset, a large ground plane and EMI shielding. Due to the large ground plane and optional side flaps it has good multipath and EMI rejection characteristics, making it a great device to be used on fully autonomous systems that need high precision and reliable GPS reception.

For more information visit the 1BitSquared [G0 pre-order page](http://1bitsquared.com/products/g0-gps).

[1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.de) has just launched their [German online store](http://1bitsquared.de/collections/all). This is the first dedicated [Paparazzi UAV](http://paparazziuav.org) hardware supplier in Europe.

With a store in Europe they can provide faster delivery times of [Paparazzi UAV hardware](http://1bitsquared.de/collections/all) for all the European users and developers. Now you can purchase [Lisa/M(X)](http://1bitsquared.de/products/lisa-mx-autopilot), [Lisa/S](http://1bitsquared.de/products/lisa-s), [Elle0](http://1bitsquared.de/products/elle0-autopilot) autopilots and accessories for Paparazzi UAV within Europe without having to pick up your order at your customs office. We hope this will make the entry into Paparazzi and working with the system easier, knowing that you can get new parts and hardware faster from a local supplier.

Check out the [1BitSquared DE store](http://1bitsquared.de), if you have any questions or suggestions let us know in the [Paparazzi UAV Gitter](https://gitter.im/paparazzi/discuss) channel, [esden](https://github.com/esden) is always there to answer your questions regarding the [1BitSquared products](http://1bitsquared.de/collections/all) and [online store](http://1bitsquared.de/). You can also reach [1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.de) through their [contact form](http://1bitsquared.de/pages/contact-us), and the [1BitSquared Gitter Discuss channel](https://gitter.im/1bitsquared/discuss).

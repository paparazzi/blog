Dear Paparazzians,

I made a [new video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPNSzWRVHuA) describing the details of the [Elle0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/elle0-autopilot) Paparazzi UAV autopilot. [Elle0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/elle0-autopilot) is now available for purchase in the [1BitSquared store](http://1bitsquared.com) together with the [G0 companion GPS](http://1bitsquared.com/products/g0-gps) receiver. In the video I go through and describe every IO pin as well as some of the design differences to the Lisa/MX Autopilot. Additionally, I describe how you can add a battery voltage monitor to your Elle0 using a voltage divider circuit.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/yPNSzWRVHuA" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

I hope this video is helpful. If you think so too please let me know in the [YouTube comments](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPNSzWRVHuA). I am planning to make many more videos describing different aspects of [Paparazzi UAV](http://paparazziuav.org), not only hardware but also flight videos and Paparazzi software tutorials and tips. If you think it will be useful for you make sure to subscribe and tell me in the comments what you would like to see.

Also, check out the [Elle0 paparazzi wiki page](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Elle0) for more information about the autopilot itself and diagrams of the system. Additionally to the [Elle0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/elle0-autopilot) and [G0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/g0-gps) boards we have also released a matching [R0 radio board and UU0 UART to USB adapter boards](http://1bitsquared.com/products/r0-radio-kit). We will be making videos describing more parts of the modular system we are working on soon. Whereas the [Elle0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/elle0-autopilot) and [G0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/g0-gps) are already in stock, the[R0 & UU0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/r0-radio-kit) are available for Pre-Order in our US store.

Cheers, and successful flights,
Esden and the [1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.com) team

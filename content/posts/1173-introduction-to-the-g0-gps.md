Dear Paparazzians,

I made a [new video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TG-2z-KdJEI) describing the details of the [G0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/g0-gps) Paparazzi UAV GPS receiver module. In the video I go through and describe the several design decisions we made to ensure high Signal to Noise Ratio, good multi path GPS signal rejection and overall reliability of the GPS for use in airborne robotics applications.

I hope this video is helpful. If you think so too please let me know in the [YouTube comments](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TG-2z-KdJEI). If you enjoyed the video make sure to like it on YouTube and subscribe to the channel.

Check out the [G0 paparazzi wiki page](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/G0) for more information about the GPS.

[G0](http://1bitsquared.com/products/g0-gps) is now available for purchase in the [1BitSquared store](http://1bitsquared.com/) together with the [Elle0 autopilot](http://1bitsquared.com/products/elle0-autopilot). It is available in both of our stores; the [US Store](http://1bitsquared.com) and the [German Store](http://1bitsquared.de). So if you live in the European union, you can now buy Elle for your next UAV project without having to worry about customs and shipping time. :)

Cheers, and successful flights,

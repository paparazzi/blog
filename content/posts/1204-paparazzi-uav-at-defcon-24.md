![Defcon24-1](/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Defcon24-1-300x135.png)We will be at [DefCon 24](https://www.defcon.org/html/defcon-24/dc-24-index.html). A yearly hacker conference in Las Vegas, Nevada. If you are there make sure to say hi. To get in touch just send a mention or private message to [@paparazziuav on twitter](https://twitter.com/paparazziuav) or a contact us in our [Gitter Discuss channel](https://gitter.im/paparazzi/discuss).

[1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.com) will be there selling their hardware. If you are interested send them a message to [@1bitsquared on twitter](https://twitter.com/1bitsquared). They will have [Elle0](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Elle0), [Lisa/S](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Lisa/S), [Lisa/MX](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Lisa/MX), [Lisa/M](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Lisa/M_v2.0) autopilots for sale as well as Lisa/S starter kits and Quad Kits. The new [Black Magic Probe v2](http://1bitsquared.com/products/black-magic-probe) programming and debugging adapter will be available at the [Hacker Warehouse](http://hackerwarehouse.com/) in the Vendor area.

See you there!

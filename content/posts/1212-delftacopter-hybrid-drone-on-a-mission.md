[DELFTACOPTER](http://www.delftacopter.nl/), an electric, long range, transitioning autonomous delta-wing helicopter, is on a mission. What mission? In this case, it's the [2016 Outback Medical Express challenge](http://mavlab.tudelft.nl/delftacopter-outback-medical-express/) and the [MAVLab](http://mavlab.tudelft.nl/) Team at TUDelft has high hopes for their hybrid aircraft.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/wj0gV08Hdr8" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

This year's [Outback Medical Express mission](https://uavchallenge.org/medical-express/) requires a UAV to pick up and bring back a blood sample of an ill-fated person called “Outback Joe” located at an inaccessible roughly known location 30 kilometer away remote location with unknown terrain.

![DELFTACOPTER](/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/delftacopter_logo.png)

Powered by [PaparazziUAV](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Main_Page), the DELFTACOPTER is also equipped with state of the art on-board stereoscopic wide field of view computer vision.

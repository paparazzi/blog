[![1Bitsy & Black Magic Probe Kickstarter](/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/1bitsybmpm_splash5_16x9-300x169.jpg)](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/esden/1bitsy-and-black-magic-probe-demystifying-arm-prog) 1Bitsy & Black Magic Probe Kickstarter

As many of you know a programmer and debugger is a tremendously useful tool for many [Paparazzi UAV](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Main_Page) based autopilots like the [Lisa/MX](https://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-mx-autopilot) and [Lisa/S](https://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-s).

Our friends at [1BitSquared](https://1bitsquared.com/) are running a [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/esden/1bitsy-and-black-magic-probe-demystifying-arm-prog) to make a new revision of their [Black Magic Probe](https://1bitsquared.com/collections/supporting-hardware/products/black-magic-probe) JTAG SWD debugger and programmer with the addition of the 1Bitsy. [1Bitsy](https://github.com/esden/1BitSy) is a development board using the same CPU as [Elle0](https://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/elle0-autopilot) and [Lisa/MX](https://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-mx-autopilot) Paparazzi autopilots. It can serve as a good prototyping platform for payload control or for your next autopilot project.

Every hobbyist and hardware enthusiast will find these boards to be an indispensable part of their tool kit. Check out their [campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/esden/1bitsy-and-black-magic-probe-demystifying-arm-prog) and spread the word! :D

Last year we had [post here about meteorological flights](/2015/12/04/multi-uav-flights-for-simultaneous-meteorological-measurements/) using multiple UAVs. Now, the video of those experiments is finally on-line.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/tLcYyohd_hE" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

In total 3 planes are used for the scientific measurements and an extra one for aerial footage of the other planes. They are all using [Apogee boards](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Apogee/v1.00) with an extra sensor board developed in-house at Enac: the MeteoStick.

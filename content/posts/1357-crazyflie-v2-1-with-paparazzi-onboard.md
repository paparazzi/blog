The popular Open-Source platform [Crazylie v2.1](https://www.bitcraze.io/crazyflie-2-1/) from [Bitcraze](https://www.bitcraze.io/) is now supported by Paparazzi. Only the main frame, with IMU, barometer as well as the NRF communication is working. A bridge between the Crazyradio and the Ivy bus allows the direct and easy connection of the Crazyflie to the ground station. More sensor decks will be supported in the future.

Now, you can have your aircraft tracking a (smooth) 3D parametric trajectory.  This is an extension of the existing GVF in Paparazzi, in fact, now we allow even 2D trajectories with self-intersections.

It is based on our accepted article in IEEE Transactions on Robotics [Singularity-free Guiding Vector Field for Robot Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.01826)

All the instructions to use the algorithm are in the [wiki](https://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Module/guidance_vector_field).

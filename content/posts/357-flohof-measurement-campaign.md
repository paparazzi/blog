[![Kerlingafjoll](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Kerlingafjoll-150x150.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Kerlingafjoll.jpg)Three Paparazzi equipped Funjets were part of the [Flohof](http://www.flohof.uib.no/) measurement campaign around the Hofsjökull glacier in Iceland. We were able to measure temperature, humidity, pressure and estimate the wind. The authorities issued a NOTAM, clearing the airspace up to 12.000 feet which allowed us to set a new Paparazzi altitude record.

[![Paul_MacCready](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Paul_MacCready-150x150.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Paul_MacCready.jpg)[Dr. Paul MacCready](http://www.avinc.com/dr.maccready.asp), legendary aeronautical engineer and founder of [Aerovironment](http://www.avinc.com/), died in his sleep at the age of 81. Many of us met him met him in 2005 at [MAV05](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Gallery#MAV05 "Gallery") in Germany but all of us are familiar with his incredible body of work ranging from the [first human powered aircraft](http://www.donaldmonroe.com/gossamer_condor_photography), to the [GM Sunraycer](http://americanhistory.si.edu/onthemove/collection/object_362.html) electric car, and the world altitude record holding [Helios](http://www.space.com/businesstechnology/technology/helios_launch_010814.html). MacCready was a world champion sailplane pilot, holds a Cal Tech Ph.D. in aeronautics, has been granted 7 honorary degrees, and has contributed a total of 4 aircraft and one car to the permanent collection of the [Smithsonian Institution](http://invention.smithsonian.org/resources/online_articles_detail.aspx?id=349). He founded Aerovironment in 1971, the world's largest supplier of hand-launched UAVs.

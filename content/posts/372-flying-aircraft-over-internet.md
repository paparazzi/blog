[![Ricou](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Ricou-150x150.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Ricou.jpg)From the [24C3 conference](http://events.ccc.de/congress/2007/Fahrplan/events/2225.en.html), Paparazzi aircraft operated in Hildesheim, Germany and in Castagnac, France were remote controlled in real time by Martin and Antoine (the station in Istanbul, Turkey was lacking a security pilot). The three local ground control stations and the remote one in Berlin were connected to a central server (located in Germany). Video from the 2 aircraft was also visible from the conference room in real-time. It was the result of great team work and ... a lot of fun. See [media section](http://www.recherche.enac.fr/wiki/index.php/Media) for some press coverage.

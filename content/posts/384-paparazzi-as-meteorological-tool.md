[![Heli_deck](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Heli_deck-150x150.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Heli_deck.jpg)Scientists from the [Geophysical Institute of the University of Bergen/Norway](http://web.gfi.uib.no/index_e.html) flew Paparazzi controlled [Funjet](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/w/images/1/1f/Funjet_spitsbergen.jpg "Funjet spitsbergen.jpg") aircrafts equipped with meteorological sensors in the Arctic sea around Spitsbergen with only the help of an RC safety pilot and no Paparazzi team member nearby. They took off and landed on the helicopter deck of the Norwegian icebreaking coast guard vessel [KV Svalbard](http://www.jtashipphoto.dk/JTA-W303%20Svalbard.htm) for one week and set a new Paparazzi low temperature record by flying at around -20°C and 15m/s wind in altitudes up to 1500m. For another two weeks they also collected data on Spitsbergen near Longyearbyen. See pictures in the gallery and a [video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VWEa_4Hlm2s).

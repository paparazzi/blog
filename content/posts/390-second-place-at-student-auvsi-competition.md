[![Targets_auvsi_08](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Targets_auvsi_08-150x150.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Targets_auvsi_08.jpg)The [OSAM](http://www.engr.usu.edu/wiki/index.php/OSAM) Paparazzi team took 2nd place in the Sixth Annual Student Unmanned Aerial Systems Competition! The event is sponsored by [AUVSI (Association for Unmanned Vehicle Systems International)](http://www.auvsi.org/home) and was held at Webster Field, St. Inigoes, Maryland. It should also be noted that this was Paparazzi's and the OSAM Paparazzi team's first time at the competition.

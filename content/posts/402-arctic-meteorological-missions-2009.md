[![Adventalen](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Adventalen-150x150.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Adventalen.jpg)[We were back in the Arctic flying Paparazzi aircrafts on Svalbard](http://blog.pfump.org/?lang=en) (N78° E15°) doing research with the [Geophysical Institute of the University of Bergen](http://www.uib.no/en/gfi)/Norway. There are two teams operating near Longyearbyen, one on the apron of Longyearbyen airport (LYR) and the other at the old northern lights research station in Adventdalen. We had permission to fly up to 1500m outside the airport opening times. The [Paparazzi aircrafts](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/SUMO) work perfectly...for humans it is just a little cold. Last night we flew having -32°C (-25°F) on the ground. There is a [video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M1k_TLcQ2ic) showing a 1500m vertical profile flight.

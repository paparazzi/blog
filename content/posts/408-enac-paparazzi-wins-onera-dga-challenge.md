[![QuadAstec](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/QuadAstec-300x225.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/QuadAstec.jpg)ENAC / Paparazzi has won the [ONERA-DGA Challenge](https://web.archive.org/web/20080113135217/http://www.minidrones.fr/), in France with a quadrotor. Twelve teams of French Universities out of 19 were
chosen by ONERA-DGA to participate in this two years contest. ENAC
designed a Paparazzi equipped [Asctec](http://www.asctec.de/main/index.php) quadrotor with an [IMU](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Inertial_Measurement_Units "Inertial Measurement Units")
developed in its lab.

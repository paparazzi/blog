The [USU-OSAM](http://www.engr.usu.edu/wiki/index.php/OSAM) Paparazzi team won the 1st place in the 7th [AUVSI (Association for Unmanned Vehicle Systems International)](http://www.auvsi.org/home) Student Unmanned Aircraft System Competition (18 teams total). The competition was held at Webster Field, Maryland, Jun. 17-20. The honor also goes to all of the Paparazzi community, especially the developers.

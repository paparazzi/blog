[![Adler_c](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Adler_c-300x200.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Adler_c.jpg)The [Institute of space systems](http://www.irs.uni-stuttgart.de/) of [University of Stuttgart](http://www.uni-stuttgart.de/index.en.html) is using the paparazzi system for large remote sensing aircraft.
The missions include basic research and environmental monitoring. Payloads of up to 7kg are carried.

More information can be found on the [Wiki page](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Adler_Uni_Stuttgart "Adler Uni Stuttgart").

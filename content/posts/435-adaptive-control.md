[![Wingdrop](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Wingdrop-300x225.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Wingdrop.png)There is a [video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TFrognLZ2Ak) available that shows how Paparazzi's adaptive control loops keep a Twinstar in the air that drops 30% of its right wing with 50% of the aileron and then also switches the right engine off. Another [video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N0H9xWckeYQ) shows a Paparazzi quadcopter using adaptive control to stay level after dropping 50% of its total weight.

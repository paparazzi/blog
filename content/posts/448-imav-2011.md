[![Enac_imav11_1](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Enac_imav11_1-300x200.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Enac_imav11_1.jpg)This year [IMAV 2011](http://www.imav2011.org/) in Delft, Netherlands went really well for all of the participants, we have seen lots of successful flights. [ENAC](http://www.enac.fr/en/home) Paparazzi Team took the 2nd place in general "Outdoor Autonomy Challenge" and [Fire Storm](http://paparazzi.enac.fr/wiki/Fire-Storm) demonstrated 105+ minutes of flight and took the "Best Outdoor Endurance Award".  He was waiting for this day for 2 years since the cancelation of IMAV 09. Additionally, Fire Storm flew its endurance mission with the new [Umarim V1.0](http://paparazzi.enac.fr/wiki/Umarim_v10) autopilot board which will be released soon.

[![Sumo_launch](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Sumo_launch-300x219.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Sumo_launch.jpg)In the Antarctic summer of 2011/2012 two teams flew [Paparazzi](https://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Main_Page)-driven UAS on the southernmost continent. [The University of Bergen](http://www.uib.no/en/node/36362) flew at the Norwegian Troll station ([video](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0T9fyCNLllI)) and the University of Colorado near the US McMurdo station ([blog](http://dl.dropbox.com/u/53700947/Antarctic_blog/blog_20120124.htm), [blog](http://alices-wonderland-adventures.blogspot.com/2012/01/uav-flights-take-2.html)). They measured temperature, humidity, pressure, infrared radiation and wind with a Multiplex Funjet plane.

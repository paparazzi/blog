[![Parrot_AR_Drone_2_TUDelft_Paparazzi](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Parrot_AR_Drone_2_TUDelft_Paparazzi-300x173.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Parrot_AR_Drone_2_TUDelft_Paparazzi.jpg)At the [TU Delft university of technology](http://www.tudelft.nl/en/) student teams made the impossible; possible. Now you can turn your AR Drone v2 into an autonomous drone, **controlled by Paparazzi autopilot**software. It's simple, Insert GPS receiver into USB port, build and upload your flight plan via WiFi, and you are done! No hardware modification needed.

[Detailed instructions to get your own drone flying with a Paparazzi autopilot brain can be found **by following this link**](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/AR_Drone_2/getting_started "AR Drone 2/getting started")

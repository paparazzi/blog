[![Weddell_sea](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Weddell_sea-150x150.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Weddell_sea.jpg)Over the last 9 weeks the Paparazzi autopilot has been used for operations with remotely piloted aircraft from the [Finnish Meteorological Institute](http://en.ilmatieteenlaitos.fi/research) for scientific research on board the R.V. Polarstern in the Weddell Sea/Antarctica. You can see the course and [read](http://blog.pfump.org/?lang=en) a short report on the homepage of the [Alfred Wegener Institut](http://www.awi.de/en/infrastruktur/schiffe/polarstern/wochenberichte/alle_expeditionen/ant_xxix/ant_xxix6/8_august_2013/).

Instructions on how to build your own small unmanned meteorological observer ([SUMO](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/SUMO)) can be found [here](http://paparazzi.enac.fr/wiki/SUMO) in the Wiki.

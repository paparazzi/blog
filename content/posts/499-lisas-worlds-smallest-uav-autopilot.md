[![2013-08-22_16.19.04](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/2013-08-22_16.19.04-300x143.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/2013-08-22_16.19.04.jpg)[TU Delft](http://www.tudelft.nl/en/) researchers in collaboration with [1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.com) have designed and build [the world’s smallest autopilot for micro aircraft](http://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-s).

Researcher [Bart Remes](http://tedx.amsterdam/speakers/bart-remes/) and his team of the [Micro Aerial Vehicle Laboratory](http://mavlab.lr.tudelft.nl/) at the [TU Delft](http://www.tudelft.nl/en/) faculty of Aerospace Engineering in a collaboration with [Piotr Esden-Tempski](http://1bitsquared.com/) from [1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.com/) have designed, built and tested [the world’s smallest open source autopilot for small unmanned aircraft](http://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-s). A smaller – and lighter – autopilot allows these small flying robots to fly longer, fit into narrower spaces or carry more payloads, such as cameras. That makes them more suitable to be used in rescue operations for example. Remes: “Our aim? Make MAVs so small and light that every fireman can fit one in his pocket.”

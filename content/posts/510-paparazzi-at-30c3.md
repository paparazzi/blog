[![30C3_small_logo](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/30C3_small_logo-200x300.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/30C3_small_logo.jpg)The [30C3 – 30th Chaos Communication Congress](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2013/wiki/Main_Page) was held in the Congress Center in Hamburg, Germany on December 27th-30th, 2013. We were present there as part of the [Paparazzi UAV Assembly](https://events.ccc.de/congress/2013/wiki/Assembly:PaparazziUAV).

Piotr Esden-Tempski of [1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.com/) made a [presentation](https://youtu.be/IePG2fzKvF0) on **Paparazzi's History** in the main events hall during the congress.

[![Penguin_logo](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Penguin_logo-150x150.gif)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Penguin_logo.gif)To make documenting and learning about [Paparazzi](https://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Main_Page) easier we have updated the [Wiki](https://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Main_Page) to the latest Mediawiki version (1.22). This new wiki provides a very improved search of the many articles, as well as video embed tags. You can easily embed a [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCXiUDV6skq_doPW5WH-eSdw/playlists) video into your article by writing {{#ev:youtubehd|videoID}}, or a Vimeo video by adding {{#ev:vimeo|videoID}}. The wiki is hosted on a new dedicated Paparazzi UAV server so it should be faster now aswell.

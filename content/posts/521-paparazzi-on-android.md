[![Google-play-badge](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Google-play-badge-300x104.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Google-play-badge.png)The Android application [PPRZonDroid](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/PPRZonDroid "PPRZonDroid"), a [GCS](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/GCS "GCS") extension for Android devices that you can use to control Paparazzi aircraft, is now available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.PPRZonDroid&hl=en).

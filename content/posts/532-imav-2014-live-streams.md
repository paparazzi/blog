[![Imav_logo](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Imav_logo.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Imav_logo.png)The Micro Aerial Vehicle Laboratory ([MAVLab](http://www.lr.tudelft.nl/en/cooperation/facilities/mav-laboratory/)) of Delft University of Technology ([TU Delft](http://www.tudelft.nl/)) is proud the present the 2014 International Micro Air Vehicle Conference ([IMAV 2014](http://www.imavs.org/)), now offering the possibility of attending the conference by live stream free-of-cost. The [live stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ahqIv6_PR_I) will support most platforms showing innovative keynotes of pioneers in the industry as well as the current state-of-the-art presented by many researchers working on MAV's. The schedule is published on the website of the conference.

[![oscon-logo](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/oscon-logo-300x82.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/oscon-logo.png)We will be showing off some of our hardware at the [O'Reilly Open Source Convention 2015 (OSCON)](http://www.oscon.com/).  The event is happening on July 20–24, 2015 in Portland, Oregon USA.

[Piotr Esden-Tempski](https://twitter.com/esden) from [1BitSquared](http://1bitsquared.com) together with [Rich Burton](https://twitter.com/epmuav) from [Hooperfly](http://hooperfly.com) will be bringing some of their Paparazzi UAV based creations to show off. If you are attending [OSCON](http://www.oscon.com/) find us in the exhibition hall for the [hardware showcase](http://www.oscon.com/open-source-2015/public/cfp/399) and say "Hi". We would love to meet some fellow Paparazzians.

If you can't find us just drop us a line on [Gitter](https://gitter.im/paparazzi/discuss) or [Twitter](https://twitter.com/paparazziuav). Hope to see you there!

[![autopilot-chip1_opt](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/autopilot-chip1_opt-300x147.jpeg)](http://www.micromanufacturing.com/content/small-autopilot-big-ideas)The Paparazzi UAV [Lisa/S](http://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-s) autopilot has been featured in [MICROmanufacturing magazine](http://www.micromanufacturing.com/content/small-autopilot-big-ideas "Small autopilot, big ideas - Lisa/S Autopilot"). We were excited to discover that the Guinness Book listing for the smallest autopilot system is for the Kestrel autopilot. Which is 14 grams heavier and several centimeters bigger than the [Lisa/S](http://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-s)!

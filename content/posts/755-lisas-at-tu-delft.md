Our friends at the [TU-Delft MAVLab](http://mavlab.lr.tudelft.nl/ "MAVLab") have made a video showing [Lisa/S](http://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-s) flying on a Quadrocopter and Helicopter. In the video you can see the [Paparazzi UAV software](http://paparazziuav.org/ "Paparazzi UAV website") compiled for [Lisa/S](http://1bitsquared.com/products/lisa-s-starter-kit "Lisa/S Autopilot product page") stabilize and control the aircraft. It is using the [SuperbitRF module](http://1bitsquared.com/products/superbit-cyrf-module "SuperbitRF CYRF module product page") to receive control commands from a Spektrum RC transmitter. All the necessary software is integrated into the master software branch of [Paparazzi](http://paparazziuav.org/ "Paparazzi UAV website").

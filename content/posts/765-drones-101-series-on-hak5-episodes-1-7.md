Piotr Esden-Tempski of [1Bit Squared](http://1bitsquared.com/) is working together with Darren Kitchen of [HAK5](http://revision3.com/hak5/) on a series about [Open Source Drones](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wBW7XXn_Bok), explaining what Drones/UAV/UAS are, how to build, operate, program, and calibrate them. This series highlights [Paparazzi's](http://paparazziuav.org) hardware and software systems and their strengths.

Here is the **first episode** in the series titled, Drones 101- Hak5 episode 1611. In this episode Piotr and Darren build an Open-Source autonomous aerial system.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/wBW7XXn_Bok" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Drones 101- Hak5 episode 1612. In **episode 2**, Piotr and Darren discuss drone basics. How they are put together, what are their components, how they operate and what makes them special.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/YZTPIS6mUjM" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Drones 101- Hak5 episode 1615. In **episode 3**, Piotr and Darren discuss how flight controllers work. Microcontrollers, sensors, input and actuators are explained. You will also see Paparazzi's [Lisa/S](http://1bitsquared.com/products/lisa-s "Lisa/S in store") and [Lisa/M](http://1bitsquared.com/products/lisa-m-autopilot "Lisa/M in store") among other autopilots discussed.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/4wTGoogBvp8" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Drones 101- Hak5 episode 1616. In **episode 4**, Piotr and Darren discuss the initial configuration and set up of the [Lisa/M](http://1bitsquared.com/products/lisa-m-autopilot "Lisa/M product page") Open-Source Paparazzi autopilot. They also look at the [Paparazzi control center](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Paparazzi_Center "Paparazzi Center Wiki page") software and [ground station](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/GCS "Paparazzi Ground Control Station Wiki page"). Demonstrating how to compile, upload and test your first autopilot firmware.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/f9AFSDn2AZU" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Drones 101- Hak5 episode 1617. In **episode 5**, Piotr and Darren assemble the Bumblebee quadcopter frame. With live construction and step by step commentary. And finally test fitting of the Paparazzi [Lisa/M](http://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-m-autopilot "Lisa/M product page") autopilot.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/fZbZTGCS4t8" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Drones 101- Hak5 episode 1618. In **episode 6**, Piotr and Darren show how to wire a Paparazzi UAV autopilot into a quadcopter, using the [Lisa/M](http://1bitsquared.com/collections/autopilots/products/lisa-m-autopilot "Lisa/M product page") and Bumblebee airframe.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/CQ6kD48WMxc" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Drones 101- Hak5 episode 1619. In **episode 7**, Piotr and Darren go through a Paparazzi UAS airframe bring-up process. Including the calibration of the IMU sensors, telemetry and ground segment architecture and much more. If you want to learn more about the [Paparazzi UAV](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/Main_Page "Paparazzi UAV website") framework this episode has a lot to offer.

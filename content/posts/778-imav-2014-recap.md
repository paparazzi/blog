[IMAV 2014](http://www.imavs.org/2014/ "IMAV 2014") was a great experience this year. If you don't know what it is, its a micro air vehicle competition and conference that was held in Delft, Netherlands.

During the three days of the conference, there were many very interesting talks and presentations as well as an incredible showcase of research talent during the competition. Here is a playlist of the talks, they can be found on the [TU Delft MAVlab YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL_KSX9GOn2P-zv9o0k04KeWSSdm2I4-Ug).

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/EW-osFoPJXg" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

One talk we wanted to highlight was presented by Gautier Hattenberger from [ENAC](http://drones.enac.fr/drones.static/ "ENAC UAV Laboratory"), he is also one of the core developers of [Paparazzi UAV](http://paparazziuav.org/ "Paparazzi UAV website"). He gave a very interesting talk highlighting the use of the [Paparazzi UAV](http://paparazziuav.org/ "Paparazzi UAV website") framework in autonomous aerial robot research.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/WENAXN-er-M" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Bart Remes gave a presentation on the Paparazzi UAV[Lisa/S and nano Quadcopters](http://1bitsquared.com/collections/hardware-kits/products/lisa-s-nano-quadcopter-kit "Lisa/S Quad Kit"). If you are interested in the technical side of the hardware you will definitely enjoy his talk.

<iframe src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/GeG1ZvdVSpc" title="YouTube video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>

If you were curious about the results of the competition, the first place was taken by a Singapore team with a great fleet of high tech quadcopters. The second place went to a German team led by [Prof. Dr.-Ing. Klaus-Peter Neitzke](http://www.fh-nordhausen.de/2713.html "Prof. Neitzke website") that flew completely FPV and the third place went to our friends at [ENAC](http://drones.enac.fr/drones.static/ "ENAC") flying Paparazzi powered quadcopters and airplanes. They also created the most usable and complete aerial maps of the village.

And finally we have to thank [Bart Remes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aRI5o4zGzl0 "TEDxAmsterdam Bart Remes talk") and the [TU-Delft MavLab](http://mavlab.lr.tudelft.nl/ "MAVLab website") team that made the whole event possible.

[![HooperFly Flying Robots](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Screen-Shot-2015-07-21-at-10.13.57-PM-300x166.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/Screen-Shot-2015-07-21-at-10.13.57-PM.png)[HooperFly](http://hooperfly.com) made a great video showing off two of their airframes, flying completely autonomously. These robotic aircraft are based on [HooperFly's](http://hooperfly.com) own rugged airframe design the TeensyFly and feature Paparazzi UAV powered [Lisa/MX](http://1bitsquared.com/products/lisa-mx-autopilot) autopilots. The [multi aircraft support](http://wiki.paparazziuav.org/wiki/GCS#Features) in Paparazzi makes these types of flights possible.
